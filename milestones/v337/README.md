## Scientific scope
- Paisley Caves directly dated pre-Clovis human mtDNA and contamination controls.
- Native-American founding mtDNA haplogroups A2 and B2 in pre-Clovis coprolites.
- Western Stemmed projectile points at Paisley contemporaneous with or slightly preceding Clovis.
- Cooper's Ferry occupation around 16 ka and stemmed technology before late ice-free-corridor viability.
- Page-Ladson occupation around 14.55 ka with artifacts, mastodon remains and 71 radiocarbon ages in an intact submerged sinkhole sequence.
- Debra L. Friedkin site pre-Clovis stemmed projectile points around 13.5-15.5 ka below Clovis/Folsom horizons.
- Kelp-highway hypothesis treated as an ecological coastal-route mechanism, not direct proof of route use.
- Explicit technology/ancestry firewall: projectile-point style is cultural evidence, not a genetic classifier.

## Evidence firewall
- Paisley DNA does not identify the exact migration route.
- Western Stemmed technology need not descend from Clovis.
- Cooper's Ferry challenges a late corridor-only explanation but does not independently prove a Pacific-coastal migration.
- Page-Ladson documents human use of megafauna but does not prove continent-scale human causation of megafaunal extinction.
- Projectile-point styles cannot be mapped one-to-one onto biological ancestry.
- Ecological viability of a kelp-rich coast is not archaeological proof that migrants used it.
