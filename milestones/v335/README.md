## Evidence firewall

- Clovis is **not** the first secure human presence in the Americas; Monte Verde II is older.
- Sparse pre-14.5-ka Monte Verde traces are **not** treated as evidentially equivalent to MV-II.
- An Australasian-affinity statistic does **not** by itself prove a separate Australasian colonization of South America.
- Early South-American cranial morphology does **not** establish a non-Native-American founding population when genome-wide ancestry does not support that model.
- The American Arctic was **not** settled only once.
- Paleo-Inuit were **not** a simple unbroken biological lineage leading directly to modern Inuit; later Neo-Inuit/Thule expansion represents a distinct migration history.
