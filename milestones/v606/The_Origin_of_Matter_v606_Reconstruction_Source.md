## Charge-2 Weyl-Dirac Composite Boron Atlas

Primary anchor: Hui-Jing Zheng, Ke-Xin Pang, Yun-Yun Bai, Yanfeng Ge, Yan Gao. Single-pair charge-2 Weyl-Dirac composite semimetals. Communications Physics (2026). Published 8 September 2026. DOI 10.1038/s42005-026-02851-w.

Core evidence: theoretical classification of all 1,651 magnetic space groups finds 14 without SOC and 10 with SOC that permit a minimal heterogeneous charge-two Weyl-Dirac pair. In nonmagnetic spinless crystals, chiral space groups 92 and 96 uniquely qualify. First-principles calculations predict enantiomorphic SDHBN-B28 boron allotropes with a charge-two Weyl point at Gamma and oppositely charged Dirac point at A as the only crossings within about 2 eV of the Fermi level. Reversing structural chirality reverses topological-charge signs. The topology yields ultralong double Fermi arcs. Symmetry-preserving c-axis strain and hydrostatic pressure retain the pair; weak a-axis strain splits the charge-two Weyl node into two charge-one nodes while preserving the Dirac point.

Evidence boundary: the material realization is a prediction, not an experimental synthesis or ARPES observation. Weyl/Dirac quasiparticles are emergent band excitations, not new elementary Standard Model particles.
