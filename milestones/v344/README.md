## Primary scientific anchors

- Gao et al., *Nature* (29 July 2026), **A global view of human centromere variation and evolution** — 2,110 complete centromeres from 65 individuals representing five continental and 28 population groups; 226 major centromere haplotypes; 1,870 alpha-satellite higher-order-repeat variants; di-/tri-kinetochore observations; chromosome-specific mutation-rate heterogeneity; evidence of archaic-hominin introgression in a subset of centromeres.
- Logsdon et al., *Nature* 629 (2024) 136–145, **The variation and evolution of complete human centromeres** — foundational complete-centromere comparison and centromeric size/alignment/kinetochore variation.
- Human Pangenome Reference Consortium Release 2 preprint (21 July 2026) — 460 haplotypes, reported >99% common-variant coverage in All of Us v8, thousands of T2T chromosomes, improved reliability and formal pangenome coordinate systems. **Status: preprint, not yet treated as a final peer-reviewed publication.**
- 1000 Chinese Pangenome, *Nature* 654 (2026) 121–130 — 1,116 diploid assemblies; 405.3 Mb of sequence absent from GRCh38 and CHM13; small variants, SVs, tandem repeats, nested variants, pan-variant eQTL and imputation resources.
- Wang et al., *Nature Genetics* 58 (2026) 664–672 — graph-native structural-variant characterization with Swave.

## Evidence firewall

- CHM13 is **not** assumed to represent the modal centromere haplotype on every chromosome.
- Centromere function is **not** modeled as sequence-only; chromatin and methylation remain integral.
- Archaic-affinity signals are **not** converted into categorical claims that an entire centromere is “archaic.”
- HPRC2 is labeled **preprint/provisional** in this milestone.
- Population pangenomes are sampled reference resources, **not biological race categories**.
- Off-reference sequence is treated as requiring graph/path-aware coordinates rather than forced linear reference positions.
