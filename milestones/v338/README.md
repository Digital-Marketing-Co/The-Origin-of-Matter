## Evidence firewall

- White Sands does **not** automatically conflict with Beringian genomic models.
- Footprints establish presence, not exact genomic lineage.
- Indigenous-American population history is not genomically simple despite a shared founding ancestry.
- Population Y/Ypykuera does **not** by itself prove a separate Australasian colonization.
- Global genomic databases materially underrepresent Indigenous-American diversity.
- Present-day low effective population size in some communities does not imply low precontact diversity.
