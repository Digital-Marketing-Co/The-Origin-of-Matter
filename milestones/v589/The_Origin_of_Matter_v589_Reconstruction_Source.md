## Dark Matter Cosmic Filament & Lyman-Alpha Atlas

Primary anchor: Yizhou Liu, Liang Gao, Shihong Liao, Kai Zhu, Yingjie Jing, Huijie Hu. *Distinguishing the Nature of Dark Matter by Mapping Cosmic Filaments from Lyman-Alpha Emission.* Physical Review Letters 137, 101002 (2026). Published 4 September 2026. DOI 10.1103/zprj-xzrj.

Core evidence: simulations predict clumpier filaments in standard cold dark matter and smoother, less structured filaments in warm dark matter. Lyman-alpha surface brightness and smoothness are proposed as observational discriminants. Upcoming approximately 30-m telescopes are identified as important for enabling the test.

Evidence boundary: this is a forecast/proposed observational probe, not a detection of warm dark matter and not a falsification of Lambda-CDM.
