## Scientific scope
v334 connects the eastern Eurasian expansion developed in v333 to the late-Pleistocene formation of Beringian and Indigenous American population structure.

### Six-level architecture
1. **Eastern-Eurasian differentiation** - Tianyuan anchors a lineage related to later Asians and Native Americans by ~40 ka.
2. **Sahul regionalization** - Australo-Papuan populations diversify after successful settlement while retaining archaic ancestry.
3. **Northeast-Asian founding ancestry** - Native-American ancestors form from East-Asian-related and Ancient-North-Eurasian-related sources.
4. **Beringian isolation** - drift and population subdivision occur during late-Pleistocene high-latitude isolation.
5. **Continental branching and expansion** - northern and southern Native-American branches differentiate as populations disperse southward.
6. **American population history** - shared founding ancestry develops into regionally distinct Indigenous populations through continuity, admixture and replacement.

## Deeper hierarchy
Tianyuan ~40-ka genome -> early East-Asian affinity -> shared Asian/Native-American ancestry -> deep Sahul founding ancestry -> Papuan/Australian differentiation -> Denisovan-related Sahul ancestry -> northeast Asian founding population -> Ancient-North-Eurasian gene flow -> Beringian isolation -> Ancient Beringian lineage -> northern/southern Native-American split -> ice-sheet/coastal dispersal constraints -> rapid continental expansion -> Clovis-associated Anzick-1 -> deep Native-American regional divergence -> South-American founder dispersal -> Holocene continuity/admixture -> later Arctic migrations.

## Primary-source anchors
- Fu et al., PNAS 110 (2013) 2223-2227 - ~40-ka Tianyuan DNA; ancestry related to many present-day Asians and Native Americans.
- Yang et al., Current Biology 27 (2017) 3202-3208 - genome-wide Tianyuan analysis and complex early Eurasian population structure.
- Malaspinas et al., Nature 538 (2016) 207-214 - 83 Aboriginal Australian and 25 Papuan genomes; deep Sahul population structure and archaic admixture.
- Moreno-Mayar et al., Nature 553 (2018) 203-207 - 11.5-ka Upward Sun River genome; Ancient Beringian population and Beringian demographic model.
- Rasmussen et al., Nature 506 (2014) 225-229 - Anzick-1 genome directly associated with Clovis and genetically closest to Indigenous American populations.
- Willerslev & Meltzer, Nature 594 (2021) 356-364 - ancient-genomic synthesis of the peopling and population history of the Americas.

## Evidence firewall
- Tianyuan is not treated as the direct ancestor of every living East Asian or Native American.
- Sahul is not modeled as genetically homogeneous after first settlement.
- Native-American founding ancestry is not modeled as a single unadmixed East-Asian source.
- Ancient Beringians are distinguished from later northern and southern Native-American branches.
- The Solutrean hypothesis is rejected by the Anzick-1 genomic evidence.
- One founding ancestry does not imply later Indigenous American genetic uniformity.
