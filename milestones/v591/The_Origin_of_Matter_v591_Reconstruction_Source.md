## Electric-Field-Switchable Chiral Phonons Atlas

Primary anchor:
Michael Grimes, Hiroki Ueda, Clifford J. Allington, Carl P. Romao, Kurt Kummer, Puneet Kaur, Li-Shu Wang, Yao-Wen Chang, Jan-Chi Yang, Shih-Wen Huang, Urs Staub.
Electric-field switching of g-wave phonon chirality in ferroelectric BaTiO3.
Nature Materials (2026), published 7 September 2026.
DOI: 10.1038/s41563-026-02737-w.

Core evidence:
Ferroelectric BaTiO3 membranes ~40 nm thick permit reversible electrical polarization control. Circularly dichroic RIXS resolves momentum-dependent phonon chirality through angular-momentum transfer between circularly polarized X-rays and the lattice. Reversing ferroelectric polarization reverses phonon angular momentum. The response has g-wave symmetry, persists after removal of the field, operates at room temperature at about 3 V, and agrees quantitatively with first-principles calculations.

Evidence boundary:
The experiment establishes electrical control of phonon angular momentum; it does not demonstrate a complete phonon computer, prove a mechanism for biological homochirality, or show deterministic magnetic-state switching.
