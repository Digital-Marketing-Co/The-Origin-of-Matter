## USbTe Spin-Textured Nematic Kondo-Lattice Atlas

Primary anchor: Yu-Xiao Jiang, Zi-Jia Cheng, Qiaozhi Xu, Md Shafayat Hossain, Xian P. Yang, Jia-Xin Yin, Maksim Litskevich, Tyler A. Cochran, Byunghoon Kim, Eduardo Miranda, Sheng Ran, Rafael M. Fernandes, M. Zahid Hasan. Observation of a spin-textured nematic Kondo lattice. Nature Communications (2026). Published 2 September 2026. DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-74841-z.

Core evidence: STM/STS in layered Kondo magnet USbTe visualizes tetragonal symmetry breaking of heavy electronic states around the Fermi level. Temperature and energy dependence show the nematic state coincides with heavy-quasiparticle formation driven by band hybridization. Spin-polarized STM demonstrates that the nematic state is spin polarized. Nature's subject summary reports the spin-polarized nematic state below about 15 K.

Evidence boundary: the experiment supports an intrinsic correlation-mediated spin-textured nematic phase tied to Kondo-lattice physics, but does not uniquely determine every microscopic interaction or prove a particular quantum-critical mechanism.
