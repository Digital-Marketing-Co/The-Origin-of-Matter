## Evidence firewall

- White Sands does not depend on aquatic-seed dating alone.
- White Sands does not by itself prove a Pacific-coastal migration route.
- Pre-Clovis sites are not treated as equal in evidential quality.
- Chiquihuite's oldest proposed occupation is not presented as universally settled.
- A sparse pre-Clovis archaeological record is not equivalent to proof of absence.
- Later widespread expansion is compatible with earlier low-density occupation.
