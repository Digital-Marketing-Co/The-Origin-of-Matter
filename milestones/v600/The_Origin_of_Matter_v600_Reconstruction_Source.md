## SrTiO3 Quantum-Paraelectric Polar-Texture Atlas

Primary anchor: Yang Zhang, Suk Hyun Sung, Nishkarsh Agarwal, Maya Gates, Cong Li, Pu Yu, Robert Hovden, Ismail El Baggari. *Imaging of nanoscale polar textures in quantum paraelectric SrTiO3*. Nature 656, 54–59 (2026). Published 22 July 2026. DOI 10.1038/s41586-026-10823-x.

Core evidence: cryogenic 4D-STEM directly images local polarity down to about 20 K. Below T_AFD≈105 K, polar nanodomains emerge; at 69 K their characteristic correlation length is xi≈20±0.6 nm. A periodic correlation at q≈0.04 nm^-1 corresponds to lambda≈25 nm. On cooling below the quantum-paraelectric crossover T_q≈40 K, periodic order weakens and the nanodomains fragment into smaller disordered clusters.

Evidence boundary: quantum paraelectricity does not mean absence of local polarization. The imaging constrains but does not uniquely establish a single microscopic theory for the low-temperature state.
