# The Origin of Matter v602 Reconstruction Source
## Structured Single-Photon Semiconductor Cavity-QED Atlas

Primary anchor: Shunfa Liu, Jiantao Ma, Hanqing Liu et al. Structured light-matter interaction in semiconductor cavity quantum electrodynamics. Nature Nanotechnology (2026). Published 8 September 2026. DOI 10.1038/s41565-026-02275-1.

Core evidence: four spectrally nearby structured modes in a semiconductor micropillar are coupled to a deterministically positioned off-axis epitaxial InAs quantum dot. Magnetic tuning selects resonances. The device generates cavity-enhanced single photons with spin-locked chiral orbital angular momentum and engineerable spin-OAM entanglement. Extended data report representative g2(0)=0.059 and 0.021, Purcell factors about 12.5 and 6.7 in a second device, simulated ~99% OAM purity near optimal placement, and collection efficiency approaching 77% for the selected component. Primary data: figshare DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.33143708.

Evidence boundary: spin-OAM entanglement is intraparticle entanglement between two degrees of freedom of one photon, not entanglement of two remote photons.
