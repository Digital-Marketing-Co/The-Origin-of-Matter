## Evidence-status legend

- **Layer I — Established:** direct/repeated evidence plus strongly tested theory. Examples: quantum electrodynamics, evolution, stellar fusion, electromagnetism.
- **Layer II — Strong inference:** convergent evidence interpreted through tested models. Examples: hot Big Bang, dark matter as a gravitating component, many deep-time reconstructions.
- **Layer III — Active frontier:** multiple viable mechanisms or unresolved microphysics. Examples: baryogenesis, dark-matter particle identity, quantum gravity, complete abiogenesis pathway.
- **Layer IV — Metaphysical:** ultimate-cause or meaning claims without a discriminating empirical test. Examples: divine first cause; why lawful reality exists.
