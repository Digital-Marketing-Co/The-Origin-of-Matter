## Shared late-milestone architecture

The late sequence repeatedly preserved a six-level scientific architecture, then expanded to approximately 20-24 levels when scientifically useful. Typical page structure included: eight substantive mechanistic sections; rendered equation plates; immediate variable/operator explanation tables; original scientific plots/diagrams; a six-level architecture page; a deeper hierarchy page; an evidence-firewall page; and a primary-source synthesis/next-frontier page.

## Evidence discipline retained across v381-v399

- Structural proximity was not treated as proof of measured flux.
- Simulation/preprint evidence was explicitly distinguished from peer-reviewed and direct experimental evidence.
- Correlation was not promoted automatically to causation.
- Species/model-specific results were not copied numerically into humans without provenance.
- Ubiquitination, cardiolipin state, calcium signaling, proton transfer, membrane curvature, and supercomplex organization were represented as state-dependent rather than universal one-variable rules.
- The mitochondrial inner membrane was progressively modeled as a coupled system of electron transfer, proton transfer, lipid composition, membrane mechanics, protein import/assembly, crista topology, metabolite transport, and ATP synthesis.
