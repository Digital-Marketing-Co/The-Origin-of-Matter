## Scientific evidence firewall

The publication repeatedly distinguishes:

- direct observation vs inference;
- peer-reviewed evidence vs preprints;
- structure vs measured flux;
- correlation vs causation;
- species/model-specific findings vs human-general claims;
- regulatory factors vs physical pore/channel identity;
- idealized stoichiometric bookkeeping vs effective cellular coupling;
- simulation-derived mechanism vs direct in-vivo measurement.

- equations must render as mathematics rather than raw LaTeX strings;

- arrows/lines must not cross or obscure text;
- tables should be centered and use the page width efficiently;
- print/PDF output should remain legible and professional;
